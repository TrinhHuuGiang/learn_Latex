
1. Paragraphs
- environment `flushleft`, `flushright`, `center` make all content inside gather  
by order `at left, at right, at middle` of the paper
- functions `\centering`, `\raggedleft`, `\raggedright` make all content next in the group  
gather order by `center, right, left`.
- `\vfill` `\hfill` using buffer full blank space for vertical or horizontal content of paper
---
2. Line
- functions `\vspace{pt/cm/...}` `hspace{pt/cm/...}` buffer a black space inline.
- `\rule{width}{heigh}` draw a line inline, set width or heigh equal 0 similar with `vspace and hspace`
